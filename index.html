Context, not just calculation

Declare the substrate — cardiogenic shock, cardiac amyloidosis, PAH, CTEPH, a valve lesion, post-transplant, mechanical support in place — and every parameter is re-read for that context, including the cases where it does not apply.

Traceable to the source

34 parameters · 235 context-specific interpretations · 591 cited sources

Every threshold and interpretation is traced to the primary literature, with the formula and citation one click from every number.

Compare maneuvers side by side

Enter baseline, a vasodilator challenge, volume loading, or a change in mechanical support as separate conditions, then read the response across them — including the interpretations that exist only between conditions.

Deterministic, and offline

Rule-based logic: the same inputs always produce the same result. No machine learning. The engine runs entirely in your browser, so it works with no signal — and no patient data leaves the device.
